The loop

It does the tedious part.
You make the decisions.

Four steps run on their own. The fifth, pressing submit, is always yours.

01

Find

Sweeps job boards and company ATS feeds, then keeps only what is fresh, in range, and in your regions.

02

Tailor

Rewrites your résumé for each posting, typeset in LaTeX, ATS readable, and gated so it never invents a skill.

03

Apply

Fills the employer's own form, including logins, one-time codes, sign-ups and embedded widgets.

04

Track

Reads the replies in your inbox, sorts them, and moves each application along its pipeline.
